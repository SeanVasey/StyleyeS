# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: 2026-01-04
**Last Updated**: 2026-01-04

## Overview

StyleyeS ("we", "our", "the application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use StyleyeS.

**Key Point**: StyleyeS operates entirely client-side. We do not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data on external servers.

## Information We Do NOT Collect

StyleyeS does **not** collect:

- Personal identification information (name, email, address)
- Device identifiers or fingerprints
- IP addresses or location data
- Usage analytics or behavioral data
- Prompts, text inputs, or generated content
- Uploaded images or reference files
- Browsing history or session data
- Cookies for tracking purposes

## Local Data Storage

StyleyeS uses browser localStorage to enhance your experience. This data:

### What's Stored Locally

| Data Type | Purpose | Retention |
| --------- | ------- | --------- |
| Selected styles | Remember your style preferences | Until cleared |
| Model selection | Remember preferred AI model | Until cleared |
| Aspect ratio | Remember preferred dimensions | Until cleared |
| Favorites | Store favorited style presets | Until cleared |
| History | Recent prompt history (optional) | Until cleared |
| Theme preference | Light/dark mode selection | Until cleared |

### Local Storage Characteristics

- **Location**: Stored only in your browser on your device
- **Access**: Only accessible by StyleyeS on the same domain
- **Control**: You can clear this data at any time via browser settings
- **Transmission**: Never transmitted to external servers

## How to Clear Local Data

### Browser Settings
1. Open your browser settings
2. Navigate to Privacy & Security
3. Clear site data for the StyleyeS domain

### In-App
1. Open StyleyeS settings (if available)
2. Select "Clear All Data" or similar option

## Third-Party Services

### AI Model Providers

StyleyeS generates prompts for use with various AI image generation services. Please note:

- StyleyeS does **not** connect to AI services directly
- You manually copy prompts to your chosen AI service
- Each AI service has its own privacy policy
- We recommend reviewing those policies before use

### No Third-Party Analytics

StyleyeS does **not** use:
- Google Analytics
- Facebook Pixel
- Any tracking or analytics services
- Advertising networks
- Social media plugins

## Children's Privacy

StyleyeS does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age. The application is intended for general audiences interested in AI image generation.

## Data Security

### Client-Side Security Measures

1. **No Transmission**: Data never leaves your device
2. **Input Sanitization**: All inputs are sanitized to prevent injection attacks
3. **File Validation**: Uploaded files are validated for type and size
4. **Secure Protocols**: When hosted, always serve via HTTPS

### Your Responsibilities

- Keep your browser updated
- Use secure, private networks
- Clear local data when using shared devices
- Be cautious with browser extensions

## Your Rights

Since StyleyeS doesn't collect personal data, traditional data subject rights (access, deletion, portability) apply to local storage only. You have full control over this data through your browser settings.

### You Can Always

- View stored data via browser developer tools
- Delete all local data at any time
- Use the application in private/incognito mode
- Block localStorage entirely (may affect functionality)

## International Users

StyleyeS is a client-side application with no data transmission. However, when hosted:

- The application files may be served from servers in various locations
- CDN caching may occur for static assets
- No personal data is processed regardless of location
