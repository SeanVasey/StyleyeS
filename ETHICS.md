# Ethics Policy

## Our Commitment

StyleyeS is committed to promoting responsible, ethical use of AI-powered image generation technology. This document outlines our ethical principles and guidelines for users.

## Core Principles

### 1. Transparency

- **Open Source**: StyleyeS is openly developed, allowing community review and contribution.
- **Clear Documentation**: We provide clear documentation about how the tool works.
- **No Hidden Functionality**: The application does exactly what it claims—no hidden data collection or tracking.

### 2. User Empowerment

- **Creative Freedom**: We support artistic expression and creative exploration.
- **Educational Value**: StyleyeS helps users learn about effective prompt engineering.
- **Accessibility**: We strive to make AI image generation accessible to all skill levels.

### 3. Responsible AI Use

We encourage users to:

- **Respect Copyright**: Only use reference images you have rights to use.
- **Avoid Harmful Content**: Do not generate prompts intended for harmful, deceptive, or illegal imagery.
- **Consider Impact**: Think about the potential impact of generated content on individuals and communities.
- **Credit AI**: When appropriate, disclose that images were AI-generated.

## Prohibited Uses

StyleyeS should **not** be used to create prompts for:

### Harmful Content
- Non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII)
- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
- Realistic violence or gore intended to disturb
- Content promoting self-harm or suicide

### Deceptive Content
- Deepfakes intended to deceive
- Fake news or misinformation imagery
- Fraudulent identity documents
- Impersonation of real individuals without consent

### Discriminatory Content
- Content promoting hate based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or other protected characteristics
- Stereotyping or degrading imagery of any group
- Propaganda promoting discrimination

### Illegal Activities
- Content facilitating illegal activities
- Imagery promoting terrorism or extremism
- Copyright or trademark infringement

## Ethical AI Model Selection

StyleyeS includes multiple AI models with varying capabilities. Users should:

1. **Understand Model Capabilities**: Each model has different strengths and intended use cases.
2. **Respect Model Terms**: Adhere to each AI model provider's terms of service.
3. **Report Issues**: Report any concerning model behaviors or outputs.

## Community Standards

### For Contributors

- Write inclusive, accessible code and documentation
- Consider diverse perspectives in design decisions
- Prioritize user safety in feature development
- Maintain transparency in decision-making

### For Users

- Share knowledge and help others learn
- Report bugs and security issues responsibly
- Provide constructive feedback
- Respect other community members

## Data Ethics

### What We Don't Do

- **No Data Collection**: We don't collect user prompts, images, or usage data.
- **No Profiling**: We don't build user profiles or track behavior.
- **No Selling Data**: We have no data to sell.

### What We Do

- **Local Processing**: All operations occur in your browser.
- **Privacy by Design**: Privacy protection is built into the architecture.
- **Transparency**: Our code is open for inspection.

## Environmental Considerations

While StyleyeS itself is a lightweight client-side application, we acknowledge that:

- AI image generation consumes significant computational resources
- Users should be mindful of unnecessary generation requests
- We encourage thoughtful, intentional use of AI resources

## Reporting Ethical Concerns

If you encounter ethical concerns related to StyleyeS:

1. **Email**: ethics@vasey.ai
2. **Subject**: `[ETHICS] Concern Report`
3. **Include**: Description of the concern and any relevant context
